## Direction contract

THESIS: Five shipped projects live behind one steel-and-glass partition, not five identical bordered cards — refuses the generic portfolio-template grid.

OWN-WORLD: Black glazing-bar grid on near-white ground; panes mostly clear/seeded-white at rest, each project's own accent color floods its pane on hover/select, a shelved/prototype project frosts translucent instead of coloring. Geist Sans for UI text, Geist Mono for pane labels/status — put to work for the first time.

STORY: Visitor reads one calm architectural wall, notices which panes glow and which stay frosted, understands range and honesty before opening any single case study, ends ready to email.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed glazing grid fills the viewport under a slim header; hero copy sits inside the wall's own frame, not a separate section above it; the flagship pane (FM Media, the live project) spans a visibly larger cell than the rest.

FORM: Glazier's color-field partition (challenger; won the direction roll against assigned candidate 4 of 7 on my own resonance-ranked list); seed key 203521ee.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
